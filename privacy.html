Privacy

This page describes how we handle information on this website and how customer evidence is treated in engagements. It is a plain-language summary, not a certification claim.

Website enquiries

If you contact us or book an assessment we hold the details you give us for the purpose of responding. We do not sell contact information.

Customer evidence

Evidence is ingested only where a customer explicitly authorizes it, and existing access entitlements are preserved. Customers decide the scope.

Deployment and control

WisdomTwin.ai deployment design targets on-premises or customer private cloud, subject to pilot acceptance testing. Air-gap is a separate unverified acceptance profile.

Model training

We do not train shared models on customer data without written customer consent.

Requests

To ask about data we hold, or to request deletion, email roman@wisdomtwin.ai. Our postal address is 90 Stadium Road, Suite 911, Toronto, ON M5V 3W5, Canada.
