How we work

The role is the unit

We model how a role decides. We do not clone people and we do not replace the person accountable for the outcome.

Governance is the product

Citations, policy checks and safe decline are not features layered on later. They are what makes an answer usable in a regulated environment.

Say only what is true

Demonstrations are labeled synthetic. Design targets are labeled as targets. We do not publish claims we cannot support.

Start with the decision that waits most often.

In one working session, we map where a recurring decision stalls today, which role holds the judgment, what evidence and approvals govern it, and what the accountable after state should be.
